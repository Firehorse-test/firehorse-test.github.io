A little bit about me…

Hi, I'm Vicki.

Personal Trainer Menopause Movement Coach Boxing Instructor

I'm 56 years old. People often assume I only became interested in fitness recently. The truth is, movement has been part of my life for over 40 years.

I started riding horses as a child and went on to qualify as a riding instructor. I discovered the gym at 16 and, like many women, I've experienced every stage of the fitness journey—from feeling strong and confident, to letting life, work and family take priority, and then finding my way back again.

Alongside that, I built a career of more than 30 years in healthcare, including many years working in mental health. Supporting people through some of the most challenging times in their lives taught me the importance of listening, understanding and meeting people where they are. It's something that still shapes the way I coach today.

Life has also thrown me some huge challenges of my own.

Losing my husband, Chris, at 44 changed everything. Strength training became far more than a way to keep fit—it became a way to rebuild. It gave me structure when life felt chaotic, confidence when I'd lost my way, and reminded me that even when life knocks you down, you're capable of becoming strong again.

Then came menopause.

The hot flushes. The brain fog. The random rage (sorry, everyone!). The changing body that suddenly seemed to have a mind of its own. I've lived it.

I know how frustrating it is when the things that used to work no longer do. But I also know that getting older doesn't mean settling for feeling tired, weak or invisible. That's why I became a Personal Trainer and Menopause Coach.

Not because I wanted to help women become smaller. Because I wanted to help them become stronger.

Today, I combine more than 40 years of personal experience with my background in healthcare, mental health and evidence-based fitness coaching to help women build strength, lose body fat and navigate menopause with confidence.

My coaching isn't about perfection or chasing the body you had at 25. It's about helping you feel stronger, move better, have more energy and confidence and enjoy the next chapter of your life.

Because I truly believe it's never too late to become the strongest version of yourself.
